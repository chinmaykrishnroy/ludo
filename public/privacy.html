Privacy & safety

Ludo Club uses guest identities. Public tables allow other players to join without an invitation. You can report or block a player from the game menu; private tables remain available for invited friends.

What is saved

  • An HttpOnly guest cookie restores your seat. Server-issued sessions expire after 30 days without activity, with a maximum lifetime of one year.
  • Offline games and display preferences are stored in your browser. Refreshing preserves progress; confirming Quit clears the offline game. Clearing browser storage also removes it.
  • Online game state is stored on the server. Abandoned unfinished games are retained for 30 days, completed archived games for seven days, and archived waiting rooms for one day.
  • Voice messages are shared with room members. Each recording is limited to 60 seconds and 1 MiB. Only the most recent 20 messages are retained per room. The default live-recording retention is 24 hours; a self-hosting operator can configure a different period.
  • Reports are accessible only to the moderator and expire after 30 days. Connection addresses are used for per-IP rate limits. Bans remain until their configured expiration.
  • Room chat keeps the most recent 50 text or GIF messages with the room, including reply references and selected player mentions. GIF uploads are limited to 1 MiB each and 64 MiB across the server. Replaced messages lose their GIF files, and deleting a room deletes its chat and media. Typing indicators are transient and are not stored. Refreshing or losing connectivity preserves the room for reconnection; explicitly leaving releases your seat. Voice retention is separate from text chat and does not depend on delivery or listening receipts.

Controls

Use Game menu → Report / Block player to report abusive conduct or hide a player's messages and prevent future matching. A moderator can review reports and block guest sessions. Because guests do not have accounts, clearing browser data loses the saved guest identity.

Operational copies

The two newest verified database backups are retained even when older than seven days. Additional recovery copies are removed after seven days or when the managed set exceeds 28 copies. Older backups can still contain recordings removed from the live database. Separately copied backups follow the hosting operator's retention policy. Browser offline caches contain the app itself; sessions, API responses, and voice recordings are never placed in the offline app cache. The hosting and tunnel providers may maintain their own security logs.

Activity counters

The homepage shows aggregate online games started, games finished, dice rolls, and active connections. These are server counters, not advertising analytics. Offline games are not uploaded for counting.
