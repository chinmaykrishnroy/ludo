# Ludo Club v0.1.17

- Hold the mic in the lobby or game to record, then release to send. The room can play up to four players' clips together, including your own. A player's board home glows while their clip plays.
- Chat stays above the phone keyboard. Incoming text, GIFs and photos get short previews on the board when chat is closed. The microphone and dice controls use equal space.
- A successful room switch leaves the previous room automatically. Online matches need two humans, and the organiser can start while other reserved players are offline. Organiser control follows join order among connected players, and the organiser can move players between colours in the lobby.
- A waiting solo lobby has 15 minutes for a guest to join. An explicit departure or room switch that leaves fewer than two humans closes the old room and clears its stored chat and voice media.

This release supersedes the unpublished v0.1.16 tag. Firefox validation uses a fake microphone supplied by the browser to exercise actual recording and playback.
