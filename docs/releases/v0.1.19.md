# Ludo Club v0.1.19

- Audio recovery on iOS Safari and WebKit: when push-to-talk voice capture finishes or is cancelled, the AudioSession route is safely restored to playback and the Web Audio context restarts seamlessly, ensuring dice, token movement, captures, and soundboard effects continue playing after microphone use.
- Missed room closure and orphaned player recovery: if all other players leave an online room or the room is closed by the server, players remaining at the table or reconnecting from background are immediately freed back to the lobby without getting stranded by room-not-found errors.
- Persistent soundboard category: the soundboard remembers the player's last selected audio category across modal dismissals and page reloads.
