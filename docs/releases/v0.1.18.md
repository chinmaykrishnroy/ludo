# Ludo Club v0.1.18

- Tap a reaction for its default effect. Hold or drag down for Pop, Burst, Float, and Ripple. Reactions start from the sender's home, leave the board visible, and use a shared countdown shown inside the reaction buttons.
- Your home stays at bottom left throughout an online game or solo practice. Pass and play keeps a fixed board for everyone sharing the screen.
- The optional server soundboard supports categorized clips, room-wide playback, local silence, and speaker mute. Sound effects quiet down during voice activity, and voice notes use automatic gain, compression, and limiting at playback.
- Mobile chat previews show the board through the message background.

Operators install their audio library on the data volume. See [soundboard setup](../SOUNDBOARD.md).
