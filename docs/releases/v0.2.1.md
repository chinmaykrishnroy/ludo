# Ludo Club v0.2.1

Patch release addressing touch interactions and gesture handling on mobile devices (specifically iOS Safari/WebKit):

- **Mobile Seat Reordering Fix**: Resolved an issue where dragging player seat tiles on touch screens (such as iPhone) caused the entire viewport to scroll vertically instead of reordering seats.
  - Replaced `touch-action: pan-y` with `touch-action: manipulation` on `.seat-card`.
  - Added explicit `touch-action: none`, `-webkit-touch-callout: none`, and `-webkit-user-select: none` to draggable cards (`.seat-card.can-drag` and `.seat-card.is-dragging`) and `.seat-drag-handle`.
  - Allowed direct tile dragging across the card body on touch devices while preserving child control responsiveness (difficulty pills and bot action buttons).
  - Expanded drag handle hit targets to Apple HIG standards (38-44px) for comfortable one-handed thumb interaction.
